NAIJAPIN iOS SETUP

1. On a Mac, open Xcode.
2. File > New > Project > iOS > App.
3. Product Name: NaijaPin
4. Interface: SwiftUI
5. Language: Swift
6. Replace the generated Swift files with the files in NaijaPin/.
7. Target > Info: add Privacy - Location When In Use Usage Description.
   Value: NaijaPin uses your location to create accurate digital address pins.
8. Choose your Apple Developer Team and a unique Bundle Identifier.
9. Test on an iPhone.
10. Product > Archive > Distribute App > App Store Connect.
